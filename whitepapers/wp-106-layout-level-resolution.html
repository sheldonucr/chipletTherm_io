ThermStack white paperWP-106November 2026

Layout-level resolution: the temperature of every cell and wire on a processor die

AbstractHot spots start at the scale of standard cells and wires, far below the 10–50 µm grids that package tools use. ThermStack APR resolves a full processor-core die down to 50 nm, with every placed cell and every routed wire of the layout in the model. The 50 nm result takes 6 min 45 s and 13 GB on one CPU server. At 1 µm, APR matches a full single-grid solve to 0.04 % at the peak with one fifth of the memory.

1 / RESOLUTION

From the whole die to single cells and wires

The whole die stays in the model; the layout detail goes where the heat is.

The design is a placed-and-routed RISC processor core: a 0.32 × 0.32 mm die with about 8,300 placed cells and nine metal layers. ThermStack reads the layout and the per-cell power directly. Every metal and via layer is taken from the drawn shapes at 25 nm, and each cell's power goes onto its own transistors.

2 / ACCURACY

The same answer as a full-grid solve

To check APR, the whole die was also solved on a single uniform 1 µm grid, which still fits in memory at that resolution. The APR 1 µm result matches it at the peak and across the whole die. Going on from 0.2 µm to 50 nm changes the peak of the layout area by less than 0.1 %: the result has converged at the layout scale.

1.4 %Largest difference anywhere on the die, as a share of the peak rise; 0.06 % rms
