Package architecture, power allocation and cooling choices are linked. ThermStack brings them into one analysis workflow, from early screening to focused investigation of the most critical regions.

01 / NUMERICAL FIDELITY

High fidelity, resolution on demand

Solve the whole package, then let ThermStack APR add resolution where the hot spots are. All eight compute dies of an MI350-class package resolve at 20 µm in under an hour and 106 GB, where a uniform 20 µm grid would need about 15 TB. FEM provides a numerical verification path.

ThermStack APR

Resolution on demand.

GPU power maps carry 20–60 µm hot spots on a 50–60 mm package. ThermStack APR (Adaptive Physical Reasoning) keeps the whole package in the model and resolves the hot spots at the resolution you ask for, on one CPU server.

20 µmResolution on all eight compute dies of a 54 × 62 mm package
56 minWall time on one CPU server, no GPU
106 GBPeak memory; a uniform 20 µm grid is estimated at ~15 TB

Synthetic power maps, not AMD data; steady state. Full study in WP-104.
